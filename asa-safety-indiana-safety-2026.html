Trivest's Safety Platform Moves Into Indiana

ASA Safety Supply, backed by Trivest Partners, acquires Washington, Indiana-based Indiana Safety & Supply — a textbook PE add-on in the fragmented PPE distribution market.

By Dhiren Dommeti · March 5, 2026 · 3 min read · Industrials · PE Buyout

Trivest Partners has a simple formula for building distribution companies: find a fragmented market, buy a platform, and keep adding regional players with strong local customer relationships. That strategy is now running in the personal protective equipment space, and the latest acquisition lands in southwestern Indiana.

ASA Safety Supply, a Trivest Partners portfolio company, acquired Indiana Safety & Supply (ISS), a Washington, Indiana-based distributor of personal protective equipment and facility safety products. Financial terms were not disclosed. The deal adds ISS's established customer base and regional distribution capabilities to a platform that has been quietly building national scale in an industry that remains highly fragmented at the local and regional level.

The PPE Distribution Market

Personal protective equipment distribution is one of those industries that rarely makes headlines but represents a substantial and recurring revenue stream. Every manufacturing facility, construction site, and industrial operation requires a consistent supply of safety gear: hard hats, gloves, eyewear, respiratory protection, high-visibility apparel. The companies that supply that equipment tend to develop deep, long-term relationships with their customers over time.

The market is highly fragmented at the regional level. Large national distributors like Grainger and Fastenal compete at the top, but hundreds of regional and local distributors serve the middle and lower tiers of the market, often with decades of customer relationships and specialized local knowledge. That fragmentation is exactly what makes the industry attractive for PE roll-up strategies — there are plenty of acquisition targets, valuations are reasonable, and each add-on strengthens the platform's density in a given geography.

Why Washington, Indiana

Washington is the county seat of Daviess County in southwestern Indiana — a region with a significant concentration of manufacturing, agriculture, and energy-related industrial activity. The area is home to operations in food processing, wood products, automotive components, and energy infrastructure, all of which generate consistent demand for safety supply products.

For ASA Safety Supply, acquiring ISS means gaining an established supplier relationship in a market that would otherwise require years of sales effort to penetrate from scratch. ISS likely brings not just revenue, but customer contracts, supplier agreements, and the kind of operational knowledge about local industrial customers that a national platform cannot easily replicate through organic growth.

"Industrial distribution roll-ups succeed when they preserve local relationships while adding the purchasing scale and operational infrastructure that regional players can't build alone."

Trivest's Approach

Trivest Partners, headquartered in Fort Lauderdale, focuses on founder-owned and family-owned businesses in the lower middle market — typically companies with $5 million to $75 million in EBITDA. The firm has built a reputation for acquiring businesses without requiring significant management change, a positioning that makes it attractive to founders who want to sell but care about what happens to their employees and customer relationships after the transaction.

That approach fits the PPE distribution market well. The businesses Trivest targets through ASA Safety Supply tend to be owner-operated distributors whose founders are approaching retirement age — the classic succession-driven sale that has become one of the most reliable deal sources in the lower middle market. Indiana, with its dense concentration of manufacturing activity and aging business owner demographics, offers a steady pipeline of exactly those opportunities.

What to Watch · Key Takeaways

  • This is a classic lower-middle-market PE add-on — small individually, but part of a deliberate strategy to build geographic density across a fragmented distribution market.
  • Watch for additional ASA Safety Supply acquisitions in Indiana and surrounding states — the roll-up playbook typically calls for multiple add-ons per year once a platform is established.
  • Trivest's founder-friendly positioning is a meaningful differentiator in markets like southwestern Indiana, where business owners value continuity and cultural fit over maximum price.
  • PPE distribution is a non-cyclical, recurring-revenue business — the kind of cash flow profile that supports significant leverage and makes exits to strategic buyers or larger distributors straightforward.
