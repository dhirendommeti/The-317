About The 317

Built in Indianapolis. Focused on the deals, markets, and companies the national press doesn't cover.

Why We Built This

The Midwest closes billions in deals every year. Almost none of it gets covered well.

The national financial press covers markets from New York and San Francisco. That lens misses most of the country — and a disproportionate share of what it misses is the Midwest. Indiana alone is home to Eli Lilly, Cummins, Steel Dynamics, Simon Property Group, and dozens of middle-market companies that move quietly and grow significantly, largely without scrutiny.

We built The 317 because we kept reading deal announcements with no context, market moves with no Midwest lens, and quarterly earnings with no local stakes attached. The region deserves coverage that treats it as the economic engine it actually is — not a footnote in a national roundup.

We're two finance students at the Kelley School of Business at Indiana University. We're not impartial observers — we're invested in this market and in the city. That's not a conflict. That's the point.

Editorial Standards

01

We verify before we publish

Every deal in The Deal Flow is sourced from public filings, press releases, or direct confirmation. We don't run rumors. If a valuation is unconfirmed, we say so.

02

We write for practitioners, not tourists

Our readers work in finance, operate businesses, or are building careers in the Midwest. We write at that level — no hand-holding, no padding, no explaining what an LBO is.

03

Regional focus is a feature, not a limitation

We cover Indiana and the Midwest because that's where the underreported stories are, not because we can't see outside it. When national policy hits local companies, we connect the dots.

04

We're transparent about what we are

We're students, not seasoned journalists. We bring analytical rigor and genuine curiosity to every piece. We'll tell you when we're uncertain and update when we're wrong.
