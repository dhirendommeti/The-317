First Financial Expands Chicago Footprint

The regional bank's acquisition of Finward Bancorp strengthens their scale in Chicago, highlighting the importance of reach in regional banking

First Financial Bancorp will acquire Finward Bancorp, Peoples Bank's parent company, in the all-stock transaction valued at $208 million. While the deal adds around $2 billion in assets and 24 branches across northwest Indiana and Chicago, it mainly allows First Financial to enhance their reach.

The deal adds $2 billion in assets, $1.7 billion in deposits, $1.5 billion in loans, and $412 million in wealth assets under management.

Finward Bancorp operates Peoples Bank, a community bank serving northwest Indiana and the Chicagoland area. With the acquisition, the entity will control roughly $4.1 billion in deposits across the new region, making it its second-largest market.

Regional banking has started to become a scale business. Larger market share allows them to spread investments across a broader customer base, recruit experienced bankers and gather resources more effectively, and build brand recognition quicker. That's the true driver behind this deal for First Financial.

CEO Archie Brown stated that the amount of regional deposits creates a platform that allows them to attract talent and support organic growth, which are more difficult to achieve with a smaller presence in the industry. Rather than directly entering Chicago, First Financial is building the scale needed to compete there.

"$4 billion at least gets us to a place where we've got a platform to grow with talent, which, when we're smaller, is harder to do. So we think we've gotten ourselves to a level we can do that now." — Archie Brown, CEO, First Financial Bancorp

First Financial has also stated that this acquisition is not a one-time event, but part of a purposeful expansion plan. Over the past 13 months, they've announced acquisitions of Westfield Bancorp and BankFinancial also, steadily increasing their footprint within the Midwest. While they work to integrate Finward, they still indicate that they are open to additional acquisitions as the opportunities emerge.

"We don't see ourselves on the sideline… There's just a window here where opportunities are popping up, and so we'll assess them as they come." — Archie Brown, CEO, First Financial Bancorp
