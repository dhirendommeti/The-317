Kanawha Scales Expands Midwest Service Platform

KSS is on a path to strengthen its Midwest footprint while diving into a broader consolidation trend across industrial services.

By Dhiren Dommeti & Ashrith Desu · July 21, 2026 · 3 min read · M&A · Industrials

Kanawha Scales & Systems announced its acquisition of Strack Scale Service, a Cincinnati-based industrial maintenance and repair service provider. While financial terms were not disclosed, it advances KSS's strategy of expanding its national network of industrial services. With the industry being known for its highly-recurring revenue businesses, Kanawha is playing the game of density.

What KSS Integrated

Founded in Cincinnati, Strack specialized in installation, maintenance, and calibration services for weighing equipment within Ohio, Kentucky, and Indiana. After years of leading this niche, the acquisition adds:

  • Established customer base across the Midwest
  • Experienced field service technicians
  • Long-term recurring calibration and maintenance contracts
  • Greater coverage in the Ohio Valley
  • Expansion for KSS' network to 28 branch locations

After the acquisition follows through, Strack will operate under its existing entity while current President Christopher Geers Jr. will remain in an executive role.

Significance of the Deal

Weighing equipment is never a one-time sale. For clients, scales are used across manufacturing, logistics, processing, transportation, and more which require consistent calibration and maintenance to meet standards and accuracy. That recurring service revenue is the key value driver in this transaction. For KSS, expanding geographically allows them to serve more customers while improving density and operational efficiency.

"The Strack Scale team has built exactly the kind of business we look to partner with - highly trained technicians, deep customer relationships, and an unwavering commitment to quality and service…" — Chris Carsten, CEO of KSS

The Platform Strategy

This deal follows a PE-style playbook, although KSS is employee-owned. The industrial services market remains fragmented, with countless regional providers only limited to local customers.

Opposed to building new branches, KSS is acquiring operators already established with customer relationships and regional reputations. This immediately strengthens their platform by expanding coverage while preserving local brands and teams.
