Blue Owl Bets on NBA Valuations

Alternative asset manager acquires minority stake in the Cavaliers at a $5.5 billion valuation, underscoring the financialization of professional sports franchises

By Ashrith Desu · July 8, 2026 · 5 min read · Finance · Sports

The Cavaliers are nearing an agreement to sell a 5% - 10% stake to Blue Owl capital, valuing the Cavaliers and Cleveland's future WNBA franchise at a total $5.5 billion. The transaction represents another milestone in today's institutionalization of professional sports ownership, where PE firms are becoming investors in some of the world's most valuable real assets.

The economics of sports have changed considerably over the past decade, as teams that were originally seen as passion assets have come to resemble alternative investments with recurring media revenues, growing audiences, and limited supply.

By purchasing this minority stake, Blue Owl gains exposure to

  1. Sports and entertainment assets related, controlled by owner Dan Gilbert
  2. Appreciation in sports franchise values
  3. An NBA franchise values at $5.5 billion
  4. A WNBA expansion team expected to start in 2028

They aren't acquiring operational control, but purchasing an ownership position to participate in passive value creation, while Gilbert stays in his position.

Why This Matters

In 2005, Dan Gilbert purchased the Cavaliers for $375 million, but the current transaction values them at approximately $5.5 billion. That's almost a 15x increase over the past twenty years, but this appreciation has also been seen in the average NBA franchise value.

  1. Average value over past 4 years → more than doubled
  2. Average value over past 15 years → over 13x

Similarly, the Boston Celtics reached an agreement to sell at a $6.1 billion valuation, and the Los Angeles Lakers agreed to a record-setting $10 billion sale. Investors believe that these valuations are not the peak, and will only continue to rise going forward.

The Rise of Sports Private Equity

Historically, league ownership excluded institutional investors, but the NBA now approves PE funds to acquire minority interests in franchises, giving them the chance to seek exposure to a previously inaccessible asset class.

Blue Owl has been active for a while already, with its sports strategy fund (Dyal HomeCourt Partners) already holding positions in:

  1. Atlanta Hawks - 6%
  2. Charlotte Hornets - undisclosed
  3. Minnesota Timberwolves - undisclosed
  4. Sacramento Kings - 5%

While demand continues to grow for these assets, the supply never changes, making it even more valuable.

The Hidden Rationale

While the Cavaliers are a major part of the deal, the WNBA team may be an even more valuable asset. With the immense growth of women's athletics through higher sponsorship spending and fan engagement, Cleveland was awarded an expansion franchise, scheduled to start in 2028. That's the main reason the valuation exceeds Sportico's recent standalone valuation of the Cavaliers, at $4.86 billion.
